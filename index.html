    LEGACY FIGHTERS DEVELOPMENT

    UPDATE LOG

    VERSION 1.81

    PLAY ONLINE FROM YOUR LINK

    • Create and join private online rooms from GitHub Pages or the downloadable game.
    • Fighter portraits now match their arena builds, hairstyles, and facial hair.
    • Both players connect to the same online arena—share your room code to play together.
    VERSION 1.8

    ONLINE ARENAS

    • Added a standalone Cloudflare multiplayer server for private two-player rooms.
    • Static-hosted editions can connect to the same multiplayer service.
    • Added isolated room storage, automatic room expiry and cross-origin connection support.
    • Improved multiplayer request timeouts, room-session isolation and reconnect recovery.
    VERSION 1.7

    COMBAT & ONLINE REFINEMENT

    • Bug fixes.
    • Added a shove control designed to break up overlapping fighters and restore fair close-range combat.
    • Reduced avoidable multiplayer input delay, improved guest-side action prediction and added a live ping counter.
    • Rebuilt Legacy Move presentation with longer, clearer and more deliberate multi-stage animations.
    • Added three independent Start Your Legacy save files.
    • Added clearer back navigation and a confirmed leave-fight option inside the pause menu.
    • Expanded the roster with two new fighters and distinct visual identities.
    • Refined the Legacy Fighters logo to match the game’s established gold, coral-red and dark-purple arcade identity.
    • Randomized the opening soundtrack across every available song. Each intro begins two seconds before its first beat drop and reveals the start menu exactly on the drop.
    • Added two new soundtracks to the game’s music rotation.
    VERSION 1.6

    START YOUR LEGACY

    • Added a persistent career mode with fighter creation, 100-point attributes, XP, levels and five skill points earned per level.
    • Added customizable existing hairstyles and body builds, adjustable height and weight, facial hair, and unrestricted colour selection.
    • Added Dojo practice in The Octagon and level-matched Arena fights on the Toronto Skyline.
    • Added tournaments every five levels with three elevated opponents and a Legacy-enabled roster boss awarding 2.5× XP.
    • Created fighters and generated opponents cannot use Legacy Moves in this version.
    VERSION 1.51
    • Bug fixes.
    • Removed fighter-to-fighter movement blocking so competitors can pass through one another and use the full width of every arena.
    • Reworked facing direction so crossing behind an opponent preserves orientation until the fighter deliberately moves back toward them.
    VERSION 1.5

    MULTIPLAYER

    • Added private online lobby rooms with shareable six-character codes.
    • Added synchronized fighter selection, arena selection and two-player combat over the internet.
    • Added host-authoritative match state, responsive local controls, motion smoothing and connection recovery to reduce lag and desynchronization.
    VERSION 1.41

    COMBAT POLISH

    • Bug fixes.
    • Power Punches and Power Kicks now miss at point-blank range while still consuming stamina, rewarding deliberate spacing.
    VERSION 1.4

    FORGE YOUR LEGACY

    A rebuilt fighter system and a new progression mode bring more identity and strategy to every match:

    • Rebuilt active combat models around articulated 2D skeletons with improved anatomy, proportions, joint motion and grounded weight transfer.
    • Added Legacy Mode with inheritable signature moves, offensive techniques and athletic traits earned after victories.
    • Added a final adaptive competitor engineered to counter the build created during each Legacy Mode run.
    • Corrected unintended body-overlay artifacts and refined combat-model rendering.
    • Preserved the previous character renderer in the source as a rollback-safe fallback.
    VERSION 1.33

    POWER COUNTS

    Knockdowns now reward true power instead of triggering automatically:

    • Made knockdown chance scale directly with the attacking fighter’s Power rating.
    • Restricted knockdowns to Power Punches and Power Kicks.
    • Kept standard strikes, quick kicks and Legacy Moves on their grounded or move-specific reactions.
    • Removed the extra tournament subtitle from the startup screen.
    VERSION 1.32

    IMPACT & AIRWAVES

    Combat reactions and title music now feel cleaner and more varied:

    • Rebuilt standard hit reactions with grounded recoil instead of vertical bouncing.
    • Added controlled heavy-hit knockdowns with distinct fall, floor and recovery phases.
    • Added four title-music tracks while keeping the original song as the opening theme.
    • Added randomized follow-up music with immediate-repeat prevention.
    • Removed the music-reactive startup-title pulsing.
    VERSION 1.31

    THE TITLE DROP

    The opening presentation now has a complete soundtrack-driven sequence:

    • Added music-reactive title motion that pulses and enlarges with the soundtrack.
    • Added a separate music-volume control connected to Master Volume and Sound On/Off.
    VERSION 1.3

    RISE OF THE CHALLENGER

    Legacy Fighters now has a complete opening flow, expanded game options and a new survival-style challenge:

    • Added a startup screen with the Legacy Fighters logo, Play menu, gamemode selection and Settings.
    • Added Challenge Mode with consecutive rounds and steadily increasing CPU health, reactions and strategy.
    • Added persistent master volume, effects volume, screen shake and fluid-motion settings.
    • Added fully customizable keyboard controls with conflict handling and a reset option.
    • Improved walking flow, body sway, hit recoil, airborne reactions, rotation and ground-bounce effects.
    VERSION 1.2

    COMBAT REFORGED

    The fighting system has been rebuilt for cleaner animation, fairer contact and more tactical matches:

    • Rebuilt hit detection with forward-facing attack ranges, active strike windows and body-size-aware contact.
    • Added separate jab, power punch, quick kick and power kick controls with distinct timing, reach, damage and stamina costs.
    • Improved striking poses so punches extend straight toward the opponent and kicks use proper knee and leg motion.
    • Added moving blocks, frontal guard detection, attack interruption and fair simultaneous hit resolution.
    • Reworked CPU strategy with delayed reactions, stamina management, spacing, pressure, retreating, varied attack choices and defensive tendencies.
    VERSION 1.1

    WORLD TOUR UPDATE

    The tournament has expanded beyond its original arena:

    • Added Stage Select after fighter selection with 10 playable fight locations.
    • Added distinct daytime, sunset and nighttime environments with city landmarks, natural scenery and specialized arenas.
    • Rebuilt every arena with detailed scenery, recognizable landmarks and greater environmental depth.
    • Rebuilt Legacy Move animations so punches, kicks, charges, strike combinations, slams, takedowns and suplexes match their move descriptions.
    • Added unique impact effects and throw reactions for different Legacy Move categories.
    VERSION 1.01

    THE FIGHTER IDENTITY UPDATE

    This update gives the entire game and roster a stronger individual identity:

    • Expanded the playable roster from 11 to 14 fighters with Beib Medoff, The Warrior and Kayess Hi.
    • Added unique builds, hairstyles, facial hair, gear, colours, statistics and Legacy Moves across the roster.
    • Improved hair and beard detail and made all accessories proportional to their body designs.
    • Removed unwanted face markings while preserving intentional masks, headwear and bandanas.
    • Added character-based stamina ratings, in-fight stamina bars, attack costs, blocking drain, recovery and guard breaks.
    • Reorganized the 14-fighter selection screen and added this update log.
